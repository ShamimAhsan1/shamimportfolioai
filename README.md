# Shamim Ahsan | AI Solutions Architect Portfolio

A cutting-edge, responsive, and animated personal portfolio website built for **Shamim Ahsan**. Featuring a high-tech cyber-luxe aesthetic, interactive neural network canvas particles, comprehensive AI service breakdowns, transparent package tiers, an interactive fillable contract statement of work (SOW) generator, and full mobile responsiveness.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Sections

### 1. 🏠 Home (Hero Section)
- **High-Impact AI Aesthetic**: Deep dark theme with cyber cyan (`#00f5d4`), electric violet (`#9d4edd`), and gold accents.
- **Interactive Neural Particle Canvas**: Mouse-reactive neural constellation particles rendered on HTML5 canvas.
- **Dynamic Typewriter**: Continuously cycles through Shamim's core specialties (AI Video, AI Photo, AI Content, AI Software, AI Web).
- **Live Metrics Counters**: Dynamically animates key achievements (`150+` Projects Delivered, `99%` Success Rate, `30 Days` Guarantee, `5 Core Pillars`).
- **Tech Stack Ticker**: Smooth infinite marquee showcasing frameworks: Python, PyTorch, GPT-4o, Gemini 2.0, Midjourney, Sora, Next.js, and Docker.

### 2. ⚡ AI Services (5 Distinct Disciplines)
- **AI Video Generation**: 4K cinematic video synthesis, text-to-video, realistic AI avatars, commercial motion design (Sora, Runway Gen-3, Kling, HeyGen).
- **AI Photo Generation**: 8K master resolution character & model synthesis, commercial product photography, custom brand LoRA fine-tuning.
- **AI Article Writing & Copy**: SEO-optimized thought leadership, technical AI whitepapers, humanized long-form articles, and sales copy.
- **AI Software Development**: Autonomous AI agents, enterprise RAG knowledge bases, custom LLM APIs, and vector search pipelines.
- **AI Website Development**: High-performance responsive web applications engineered with Next.js 14/15, React, and embedded AI chatbots.
- **Interactive Filters & Modals**: Filter by category (Multimedia, Development, Content) and open detailed technical specification modals with 1-click "Add to Contract" functionality.

### 3. 💎 Package Price (3 Tier Model with 30-Day Guarantee)
- **Standard Package ($300 / 30 Days)**: Entry-level essential scope. 1 primary AI service, 15 AI photos or 5 videos, 4 SEO articles, 2 revision rounds, standard email support.
- **Pro Package ($550 / 30 Days)**: Most popular scaling tier. Up to 3 combined AI services, 40 photos, 15 videos, 10 SEO articles, multi-page app layout, 5 revision rounds, priority WhatsApp support.
- **Studio Package ($50 / 30 Days - Maximum Offer)**: The ultimate studio suite with maximum offer deliverables unlocked as requested. Includes **all 5 AI services simultaneously**, unlimited videos & photos, complete SEO article engine, enterprise AI software, full-stack website development, unlimited revisions, and 24/7 dedicated VIP hotline.
- **Interactive Pricing Matrix**: Collapsible side-by-side feature comparison table.
- **Billing Frequency Switcher**: Toggle between Monthly Dedicated and 30-Day Sprint SOW.

### 4. 📝 Contract Us (Fillable Project Agreement Form)
- **Official SOW Generator**: Interactive agreement form capturing Client Name, Business Email, Company, Phone, Selected Package, and Desired AI Services.
- **Live Document Sync**: As the client types and selects options, a high-tech **Live Statement of Work (SOW)** document card updates immediately in real time.
- **Authorized Digital Signature**: Signee inputs their legal name as digital signature with timestamp.
- **Terms Agreement**: Legally structured 30-day service guarantee and milestone delivery terms.
- **Execution Modal & Printable SOW**: Validates form inputs, triggers an execution modal with a formal reference ID (e.g., `SA-2026-OCT-8429`), and offers 1-click printing/PDF export.

### 5. 👨‍💻 About Us (Shamim Ahsan)
- **Founder & Lead AI Engineer**: Professional biography highlighting Shamim's vision, philosophy, and technical track record.
- **Specialization Meters**: Animated proficiency bars across Generative Media (98%), AI Software (95%), Full-Stack Web (96%), and SEO Content (92%).
- **Milestone Highlights**: Production AI deployments and 30-day agile sprint delivery methodology.
- **Direct Contacts**: Verified email link (`contact@shamimahsan.ai`), WhatsApp direct, and social media channels.

## 🎨 How to Customize

- **Change Contact Details**: Open `index.html` and search for `contact@shamimahsan.ai` or update the WhatsApp phone number in the direct channel link (`https://wa.me/YOUR_PHONE_NUMBER`).
- **Adjust Package Prices**: In `index.html`, update the prices in the `<span class="price-val" data-base="300">300</span>` attributes and corresponding radio values.
- **Deploy Online**:
  - **Vercel**: Drag and drop this folder onto [vercel.com/new](https://vercel.com/new).
  - **Netlify**: Drag and drop the folder onto [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
  - **GitHub Pages**: Push this directory to a GitHub repository and enable Pages in repository settings.
